Data

Cache keeps all notes on your computer, in the workspace folder you choose. It sends nothing anywhere on its own. Only when you paste or drop a web link or a web image does it fetch that page or image, to show its title or save the picture with the note. Teams, mail and other programs get a note only when you copy it. This website loads no fonts or scripts from third parties; to show the newest version it asks GitHub's public API.
